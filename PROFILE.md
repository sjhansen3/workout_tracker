# Athlete profile

Edited through coaching conversations only. Every line here is something Steve said.

- IRONMAN 70.3 Wisconsin, Madison. Saturday 11 Sep 2027 (likely date; not registered yet). Goal for this cycle: get fit, finish, enjoy it.

## Life
- Two young kids. Works from home. Steve: "most important thing to know is this." His fitness goals are about staying on top of that life.
- Weekdays: routine short sessions ("let's just do 30 min") at night after the kids' 8pm bedtime. Midday also works and is a good work break, but check his calendar first. Big/long sessions are best midday. Weekends: nap time / midday window. Usually sleeps 7+ h, but sleep can be the blocker.
- Daily push at 9am, not 6am.
- Family/social plans and sleep/fatigue are what knock training off track.

## Constraints
- Back: overworking legs leaves his legs or back tight. "Not too bad." Keep leg volume moderate in strength work and avoid heavy legs right before runs.
- No days are off-limits. The Tuesday "Trainer" on the calendar is an old trainer, not a session.
- Available training time now: under 5 hours per week.
- Winter block priority: balanced strength and endurance.
- No pool access until spring.
- Location: Evanston, IL (60201).
- Weather rule: cold is fine; wet is not. Run outside when precipitation chance is under 40%; otherwise treadmill. Dark is OK: he has lights. Has a treadmill at home. Snow on the ground counts as wet unless Steve says otherwise. Will train through temps well below freezing.

## Strength, how he relates to it
- "I have never been one for lifting, but it helps so much with stress." Endurance person by identity; converted by trainers who didn't do "bro style" lifting. "I'm into it now."
- Scared of loaded back squats and the like. No barbell back squat on the plan; goblet, split squat, hinge and carries do the job.
- What strength is for this year: durability for the race, and the stress relief. Not chasing numbers.

## Preferences
- Hardest part is deciding what to do and where. Can be lazy and want to just chill. So: the plan decides for him, one clear thing per day.
- Over-tracking and over-analysis are a pitfall. "Best to just lace up the shoes and see how we feel." Keep numbers out of his way.
- "Be kind to myself instead of hard ass."
- Supersets: "I usually can get more done faster if we super set (but don't overdo that)." Pair non-competing exercises; two or three pairs a session, not everything.
- Sauna is his favourite: "such a nice reward." Pair it with sessions as the payoff.
- Wants regular massage with Nicole, his massage therapist; timing coordinated with her.
- Likes how Runna works: one workout, pushed to the watch, adapts on its own, easy to move.
- Wants low friction: look, do it, or ask for a change, and go.
