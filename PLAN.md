# The year

46 weeks from Mon 26 Oct 2026 to race day. Under 5 hours a week to start, growing only where the bike demands it.

## Phase 1 — Habit and strength (26 Oct – 31 Dec, 10 weeks)
2 × strength (Hevy), 2 × easy run, 1 × bike (once the KICKR is live). About 4–4.5 h.
The goal is consistency, not fitness. Runs stay easy. No intensity until the habit is boring.

**Sub-goals by 31 Dec**
- 10 consecutive weeks with 4 or more sessions logged.
- KICKR set up, calibrated, TrainerRoad connected, one FTP ramp test done.
- 15 easy miles a week feels normal.

**Checkpoint, mid-December:** choose how phase 2's structured bike workouts get executed (intervals.icu + Zwift, TrainerRoad, or other). Rule: the coach must be able to schedule it, or it plans around one app's calendar. Decide with the KICKR set up and an FTP number in hand.

## Phase 2 — Engine (1 Jan – 31 Mar, 13 weeks)
TrainerRoad low-volume plan (3 rides, ~3 h) is the spine. 2 runs, one growing to 8–10 mi. Strength 1–2×. Hours drift to 5–6.
The bike is where a runner's 70.3 is won or lost. This block buys that.

**Sub-goals by 31 Mar**
- Second FTP test, number went up.
- 90 min indoor ride is comfortable.
- 10-mile long run done.
- Strength still happening at least once a week.

## Phase 3 — Three sports (1 Apr – 30 Jun, 13 weeks)
Pool opens: 2 swims, building to 1500 m continuous. Bike moves outdoors on weekends. Structured run build starts around early May (16–20 weeks out); decide then which app runs it, on the condition that the coach can schedule it. Sprint or Olympic tri in June as a rehearsal.

**Sub-goals by 30 Jun**
- 1500 m continuous swim.
- 40-mile outdoor ride.
- One tune-up race finished.

## Phase 4 — Specific (1 Jul – 11 Sep, 10 weeks)
Long rides to 3 h, bricks, open-water swims, 2-week taper.

**Sub-goal**
- Finish. Enjoy it.

## Rules the coach follows
- Never add intensity and volume in the same week.
- A missed session is marked, never deleted, never "made up" by stacking.
- One hard day per sport per week, maximum, in phases 2–4. Zero in phase 1.
- Every fourth week is lighter by about a third.
- When Steve says he's not enjoying something, the plan changes, and the decision is logged with his words.
- Weather moves days, not just venues: a dry 45° Thursday in January is worth moving a strength day for.

## Strength, across the year
Why: runners' 70.3s fall apart on the bike and in the last 5 km of the run. Strength buys durability there, and protects the back and hips that tighten when legs are overworked.

**Phase 1 (now):** 2 × full body a week, Hevy, ~35–45 min, at home. Two sessions that alternate, A and B, so each lift comes round once a week:
- A: squat pattern (goblet squat), horizontal pull (one-arm row), horizontal push (dumbbell bench/floor press), glute bridge, overhead press, plank, dead bug.
- B: hinge (dumbbell Romanian deadlift, light), split squat with the rear foot on the bench, band-assisted pull-ups and band rows off the bar, incline dumbbell press, band pull-aparts, ball hamstring curl, side plank, bird dog. Ends with 5 min of thoracic and hip-flexor mobility (desk posture).
- Rules from intake: two pulls per push (left shoulder, desk posture); presses neutral-grip; no loaded back squat yet, but it's a destination (goblet → dumbbell front squat → barbell when a bar and rack exist), not a ban.
- Three supersets plus a core finisher. Legs moderate: 3 sets, never to failure, and never heavy the day before a run.
- Progression: when every set hits the top of the range with 2–3 reps in hand, add 5 lb (upper) or 10 lb (lower) next time. Every 4th week, one set fewer everywhere.
- The first session calibrates weights; the coach sets them from the second on, read back from Hevy.

**Body:** ~170 lb by April from 180, through training and one food lever at a time (first: a real breakfast with protein). No calorie counting, weigh-in at most weekly.

**Phase 2:** 1–2 × a week, the same A/B, held at the weights reached. The bike is the priority; strength never leaves the week.
**Phases 3–4:** 1 × a week, 30 min, same lifts lighter, more single-leg and core. Last strength session two weeks before the race.
